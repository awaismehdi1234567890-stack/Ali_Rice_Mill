About Us

Founded in 1970, Ali Rice Mill has been a cornerstone of quality rice production in Pakistan. As a family-run business, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to deliver premium rice varieties that meet international standards.

Our state-of-the-art facility processes over 100,000 tons of rice annually, ensuring freshness and purity from paddy to package. With a legacy spanning generations, we pride ourselves on sustainable farming practices and community support.
